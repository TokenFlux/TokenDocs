# Referral Rewards

Invite friends to register on TokenFlux and earn inference credits based on your rebate rate (default is **20%**) on every top-up they make.

**Go to the referral page: [https://tokenflux.dev/affiliate](https://tokenflux.dev/affiliate)**

## How to Invite

1. Open the [referral page](https://tokenflux.dev/affiliate).
2. Copy your **affiliate code** or **invite link** (in the form `https://tokenflux.dev/register?aff=YOUR_CODE`).
3. Share it with a friend. They register through the link, or enter your code during registration.

::: warning The link is only bound at registration
The referral relationship is established at the **moment your friend registers**. It cannot be added afterwards and cannot be changed. Users who already have an account cannot be linked as invitees.

Referral cookie tracking is valid for **30 days**. If your friend opens the link but registers more than 30 days later, the referral is lost.
:::

## Reward Rules

| Item           | Rule                                                                                                                |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rebate rate    | Default **20%** of your friend's top-up (individual accounts may be adjusted, as shown in "My Rebate Rate" on page) |
| Payout timing  | Credited as soon as the top-up completes                                                                            |
| Per-friend cap | Up to `1000🍥` **from each friend**                                                                                 |
| Validity       | No expiry                                                                                                           |

The cap applies per invitee independently. Once cumulative rebates from a single invitee reach `1000🍥`, subsequent top-ups by that user no longer generate rebates, without affecting rebates from other users. If a rebate exceeds the remaining allowance for that invitee, only the remaining amount is credited.

## Claiming Rewards

Rebates do **not** go straight into your account balance. They land in an "available rebate quota" first. Click `Transfer to Balance` on the referral page to convert it into spendable balance.

- The conversion is 1:1.
- Each transfer moves the **entire** available amount; you cannot pick a partial amount.
- Once transferred it behaves like any other balance and can be spent on anything.

- **Available rebate quota**: what you can transfer right now
- **Historical rebate quota**: the running total you have ever earned
- **Frozen**: amounts still in a waiting period (hidden when there is no freeze period)

## Which Top-Ups Count

- Your friend's **balance top-ups** and **subscription purchases** both count.
- **Redemption codes** count as well.
- Balance added manually by an administrator does **not** count by default.

## Notes

- In the invited users list, friend emails are masked (for example `a***@e***.com`), and at most 100 records are shown.
- Rebate quota can only be moved into your site balance. **Cash withdrawal is not supported.**
- Do not farm rebates through bulk registration or fake identities. Doing so violates the [Usage Policy](/en/docs/tos/usage-policy), and accounts may be banned without refund.
